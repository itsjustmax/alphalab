The pre-open brief: one card the member reads with coffee. Write
`widgets/brief` fresh (replace yesterday's) with today's truth:

- What changed overnight against OUR trades: news receipts, gaps in
  the quotes, anything touching a thesis or invalidation.
- The regime: what the gamma/zone cards in your slice say about today's
  tape character.
- The desk's state in one line each: trades by state, plans by status
  (DRAFT waiting on inspection? active bots watching?), anything armed
  (live is the member's domain — report, never act).
- The one thing that needs the member's eyes first.

Clocks with every claim. If a data lane is dark (pre-market nulls,
a stale stream), the brief SAYS so rather than papering over it.

HANDOFF: the member starts their day here; ask cards for any decision
you need. Your say is one sentence: the day's headline for this desk.

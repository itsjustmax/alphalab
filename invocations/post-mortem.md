A trade cycle closed (or a thesis retired) — the arguments carry
`trade_id`. Close the learning loop, honestly:

1. Read the cycle from the trade's `executions`: entry, exit, the
   plan's stop/target at the time, what actually happened.
2. Judge the PROCESS separately from the OUTCOME: a loss inside the
   plan is a good trade; a win outside it is a bad one. Say which this
   was.
3. Write the lesson as `findings/lesson-<trade_id>-<n>`:
   {title, source: "post-mortem", as_of, and a body naming what to
   repeat or change — specific enough that a future plan could encode
   it}.
4. If the plan's own words or parameters were the weak point, say so
   plainly — the member decides whether the next cycle's plan changes.

HANDOFF: lessons feed originate (what to look for) and compile-plan
(what to encode). The plan library records the judged outcome by
itself — you write the WORDS, the machinery keeps the score. Never
soften a loss; the desk learns from named failures only.

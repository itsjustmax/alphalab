The research funnel's intake: one sweep for NEW trade ideas from
receipts. Read the news lanes, the gamma/regime cards, and the quotes
in your slice; look for a driver with a clock — a catalyst, a regime
shift, a structural change — that is not already covered by an
existing trade.

For each genuinely new idea (0-2 per sweep, never more):
- Write the driver as a finding: `findings/<id>` {title, source, as_of}.
- Stage the idea contract-less through the forms lane:
  `forms/trade/<short-id>` {"contracts": [], "thesis": "...",
  "invalidation": "..."} — the thesis names the driver and its clock;
  strikes come LATER from chain receipts, never from this sweep.

HANDOFF: the workshop and scan-contracts lanes take ideas from here to
candidates; the member advances them. If nothing on the desk supports
a new idea, say "no new drivers this sweep" — restraint is a result.
Never duplicate an existing trade's thesis; sharpening one belongs to
its workshop.
